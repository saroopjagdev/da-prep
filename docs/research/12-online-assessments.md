# Online assessments (Phase 1.3)

Research date 2 October 2026. Builds on `02-assessment-formats.md` (keep reading that too). Labels: OFFICIAL (vendor or employer), MULTI-REPORT, SINGLE-REPORT, JUDGEMENT. Seen via web-search summaries only; vendor pages could not be opened directly.

**Caution:** prep sites disagree, often copy each other, and sell practice packs. Several summaries contradict each other on vendor (e.g. Deutsche Bank: SHL vs Cappfinity). Vendors also change: PwC reportedly moved from Arctic Shores to SHL in 2025; EY moved to a single Cappfinity "EY One Assessment" in 2025. Treat vendor-per-firm as "most recently reported", not fixed.

## D. Common mistakes and best strategies

| Point | Who says so | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Read the question before the chart; charts contain distractors | Prep sites (echoing SHL guidance) | MULTI-REPORT | [Psychometric Success](https://psychometric-success.com/aptitude-tests/test-types/shl-numerical-reasoning-test) |
| Guess rather than leave blank (no negative marking reported on SHL) | Prep sites | MULTI-REPORT | as above |
| Use SHL's own practice tests first (SHL Direct / candidate site) | SHL | OFFICIAL (via summaries) | [PracticeAptitudeTests SHL](https://www.practiceaptitudetests.com/resources/shl-style-tests-guide/) |
| For time-recorded Cappfinity tests, speed counts: work quickly and accurately; read your instructions for permitted tools | Cappfinity guidance (via summary) | OFFICIAL (via summary) | [PracticeAptitudeTests Cappfinity](https://www.practiceaptitudetests.com/testing-publishers/cappfinity/) |
| True/false/cannot say: "cannot say" means the text gives not enough information; don't use outside knowledge | Prep sites | MULTI-REPORT | [PracticeAptitudeTests Aon](https://www.practiceaptitudetests.com/testing-publishers/cut-e/) |
| Percentages and percentage change, ratios, currency conversion and reading units ("in £000s") are the most common numerical traps | Prep sites | MULTI-REPORT | [JobTestPrep SHL answers](https://www.jobtestprep.co.uk/shl-numerical-test-answers) |
| SJTs and personality: answer consistently and in line with the firm's published values, but honestly (forced choice catches faking) | Prep sites | MULTI-REPORT | [AssessmentCentreHQ OPQ](https://www.assessmentcentrehq.com/opq-test/) |
| Games (Arctic Shores, Pymetrics): no right answers; be consistent, follow instructions, play in a quiet place; practice rounds only unlock inside the real assessment | Prep sites | MULTI-REPORT | [PracticeAptitudeTests AS](https://www.practiceaptitudetests.com/testing-publishers/arctic-shores/) |
| Do tests promptly: UBS gives 7 days, BlackRock HireVue 5 days, GS HireVue 24-48 h reported | Firms/prep | MULTI-REPORT | per-firm files |

## E. Gaps in our replicas (vs this research) (JUDGEMENT; input to Phase 2)

Existing replicas: SHL-style numerical, inductive and deductive; Aon scales numerical (short form) and verbal; SJT most/least, rate-each, ranking; OPQ-style forced choice; Likert work-style.

Missing, in rough order of finance value:
1. **Cappfinity-style tests** (numerical with ranking/free-entry answers; verbal with fill-the-gap; critical reasoning; time-recorded mode). HSBC, EY, Deloitte, some Barclays/Lloyds/JPM.
2. **HireVue-style video practice** linked to the mock interview (the main GS/JPM/MS/BofA/BlackRock screen). Check how the current interview feature handles timing.
3. **Job simulation** (inbox + scenario, mixed question types): HSBC, KPMG, Deloitte, EY.
4. **Full-length Aon scales** (37 numerical / 49 verbal in 12 min; ours are 18 items).
5. **SHL Verify verbal / "Working with Information"** (NatWest, Lloyds).
6. **Watson Glaser-style critical thinking** (some finance and law; Big 4 routes).
7. **Aon switchChallenge-style** (Morgan Stanley).
8. Talent Q Elements style (Citi/BoE reports), CCAT, Saville Swift: lower priority.
9. Games (Arctic Shores, Pymetrics): explain only; don't simulate (no right answers, and imitation risks misleading students).
10. Coding (HackerRank/Codility): link to the free official practice platforms rather than build our own.

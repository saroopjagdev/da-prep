# Users: what students struggle with (Phase 1.7)

Research date 2 October 2026.

## Method and its limits (please read)
- **Reddit could not be searched at all**: the search tool is blocked from reddit.com, and direct fetches are blocked in this session. TikTok and YouTube comments can't be searched either.
- The Student Room (TSR) and Wall Street Oasis (WSO) threads could be found by search, but **only as search-engine summaries**. The "quotes" below are therefore **paraphrases of what the summary says the thread contains, not verbatim quotes**. Before using any as a quote on the site, open the thread and copy the exact words (and check it's OK to quote).
- Survey data (UCAS, Sutton Trust, ISE) is the stronger evidence and is used to rank.
- **Ranking is JUDGEMENT**, weighing survey evidence first, then how often a theme came up across separate threads and searches.

## Paraphrased voices (from search summaries; verify before quoting)
- A student who applied to over 30 degree apprenticeships: 19 video-interview invites, 16 completed, 6 rejections so far and no response from the rest. ([TSR](https://www.thestudentroom.co.uk/showthread.php?t=7558431))
- An applicant who did a finance apprenticeship assessment before Christmas still had no reply, and wondered whether companies "just ghost" the people they reject. ([TSR search summary](https://www.thestudentroom.co.uk/showthread.php?t=7578133))
- GS applicants describe applying in early October and staying "under review" for months, as Goldman works through batches. ([TSR GS](https://www.thestudentroom.co.uk/showthread.php?t=7636996))
- CV advice from a TSR reply: as it's an apprenticeship, "they know you're not going to have much/any experience". ([TSR CV](https://www.thestudentroom.co.uk/showthread.php?t=6602180))
- WSO view: most bank apprenticeships sit in operations or middle office, so moving into IB from them "would be pretty hard". (Partly outdated; see rank 8.) ([WSO](https://www.wallstreetoasis.com/forum/investment-banking/investment-bank-degree-apprenticeships))

## What this means for the site (JUDGEMENT)
1. **A guided "season plan"**: what to do each month (Year 12 summer prep, September applications), with the live calendar from `10-market.md`. This answers pain points 1, 2 and 11.
2. **A multi-application tracker with real opening/closing dates and reminders** (2).
3. **"What happens next and how long it takes" per firm**, including batch processing and typical silence, to reduce anxiety (3).
4. **HireVue practice in the real format with confidence-building feedback** (4) and **test practice in the real provider style** (5) remain the core paid value.
5. **Commercial awareness made simple**: a weekly 5-minute brief written for 17-year-olds, plus "why this firm" packs from per-firm news (6).
6. **"Your experience counts" STAR builder** using school, jobs, sport and volunteering (7).
7. **Myth-busting pages**: which finance DAs are front office, which firms don't offer DAs, apprenticeship vs university trade-offs (8, 9). Good for SEO too.
8. **A clear AI-use policy page** for students and parents: we coach, we don't write; how firms view AI-written applications (10).

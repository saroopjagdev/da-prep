# HireVue and video interviews (Phase 1.4)

Research date 2 October 2026. Labels as elsewhere. Seen via search summaries. Questions below are **reported** (paraphrased from public candidate/prep reports); we must not copy any firm's private question bank. For practice, the app should use original questions in the same style.

## 1. How one-way video interviews work (HireVue)

| Fact | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Most video assessments have **5 to 8 questions** | OFFICIAL (HireVue FAQ, via summary) | [HireVue interview tips](https://www.hirevue.com/candidates/interview-tips), [HireVue: how to take](https://www.hirevue.com/blog/candidates/how-to-take-a-hirevue-interview) |
| Thinking time usually **about 30 seconds** (employers set 30 s to 2 min); answer time **up to 3 minutes** (employer sets it) | OFFICIAL (via summary) | as above |
| **One practice question** you can redo as often as you like; real questions have employer-set retakes (often none) | OFFICIAL (via summary) | as above |
| Employer sets question count, think time, answer time and retakes | OFFICIAL (via summary) | as above |
| Jan 2021: HireVue **stopped facial analysis** after an independent audit; visual analysis added almost nothing to prediction. AI scoring (where used) is on the **transcript** against an employer rubric | MULTI-REPORT (SHRM and others) | [SHRM](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/talent-acquisition/hirevue-discontinues-facial-analysis-screening) |
| Claims that HireVue scores "vocal tone" or "STAR structure" come from prep blogs, not HireVue | SINGLE-REPORT (unverified) | [SolidAITech](https://www.solidaitech.com/2026/04/how-to-beat-ai-video-interviews-hirevue.html) |
| Many firms have humans watch recordings; whether a given bank uses AI scoring is rarely stated publicly. GS describes its first-round as "AI-assisted" (prep site wording) | SINGLE-REPORT | [GameAssessmentPrep GS](https://www.gameassessmentprep.com/employers/goldman-sachs) |

## 4. What good answers look like

| Guidance | Label | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Use STAR; spend most of the answer on Actions (one guide: S 20%, T 10%, A 60%, R 10%) | MULTI-REPORT | [Thita](https://thita.ai/blog/interview/hirevue-interview-preparation-complete-guide), [Wikipedia STAR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Situation,_task,_action,_result) |
| Aim for 1 to 2 minutes even when 3 are allowed | MULTI-REPORT | [Management Consulted](https://managementconsulted.com/hirevue-interview/) |
| Quantify results where possible; say "I", not "we" | MULTI-REPORT | as above |
| "Why this firm" should be specific: name the division, a real deal or strategy, and link it to your own story, not prestige | MULTI-REPORT | [Interview Guys MS](https://blog.theinterviewguys.com/morgan-stanley-hirevue-questions/), [Interview Guys BofA](https://blog.theinterviewguys.com/bank-of-america-hirevue-interview-questions-answers-2026/) |
| Look at the camera; practise in one take with a timer | MULTI-REPORT | [Management Consulted](https://managementconsulted.com/hirevue-interview/) |
| Use the firm's own values words where true (e.g. GS partnership/client service/integrity/excellence; MS "do the right thing") | JUDGEMENT | per-firm values in `finance-firms/` |
| For school leavers, examples can come from school, part-time jobs, sport, volunteering, clubs; firms don't expect work experience | JUDGEMENT | — |

## 7. Best ways to stand out (JUDGEMENT)
1. **Own "school leaver + finance degree apprenticeship"**. Intervyo and Trackr are built for university students; Mockly/Hugo aren't finance-deep. Nobody combines firm-accurate finance processes with school-leaver-level coaching.
2. **Honesty as a feature**: every fact labelled and sourced; "who doesn't run DAs" pages; no "leaked questions". This builds trust with parents and teachers, who often pay or recommend.
3. **Season pass pricing** (like Intervyo £139/year and Trackr £30/season) suits how students actually apply (Sept to March).
4. **Live season calendar with alerts** for DA openings (Trackr's strength), at least for the ~30 finance firms.
5. **Schools channel**: Unifrog-style licences show schools pay about £3k/year for careers platforms; a school/class licence could undercut that and reach students free.
6. **Parent-friendly** explanations and safeguarding (Children's Code) as a differentiator vs adult-focused AI tools.
7. Partnerships with free charities (Pathway CTM, Sutton Trust, Young Professionals) rather than competing with them.

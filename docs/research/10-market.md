### Most competitive and most wanted, by sector (JUDGEMENT, from the above plus firm research)
1. **Investment banks' front office** (GS FICC & Equities, BofA Global Markets, DB IBCM, UBS Global Markets, Rothschild Global Advisory, CIBC): smallest intakes, biggest brands, highest pay.
2. **Big 4 audit and tax degree routes and PwC Flying Start**: much larger intakes, very high application volumes.
3. **Bank technology DAs** (GS Engineering, JPM DTS, DB TDI, MS Technology): larger intakes, need Maths or Computing.
4. **Retail and commercial banking** (HSBC, Lloyds, NatWest, Santander, Barclays): more places and more locations outside London.
5. Outside finance: the Digital & Technology Solutions Level 6 standard is the most common school-leaver DA (600+ 18-year-old starters, as reported).

**Key message for students (JUDGEMENT):** for investment banks, apply in **September and October of Year 13**. Prepare in Year 12 (spring and summer). Asset managers and regulators come later (January to March).

**What it means (JUDGEMENT):** finance-only is a niche of roughly 35-50k students at a time, about half of them in Year 13 during the season. At 5% paid conversion that is about 2,000 paying users, so finance should be the *flagship* rather than the whole market. The same product serves tech, Big 4 and engineering DAs (already in the app), which multiplies the audience several times.

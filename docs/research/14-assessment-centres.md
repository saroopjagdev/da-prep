| Exercise | What happens | What assessors look for | Label | Source |

## 3. Technical questions school leavers may get (JUDGEMENT from the above)

Graduate guides list DCF, WACC, LBO and accretion/dilution ([IGotAnOffer](https://igotanoffer.com/en/advice/investment-banking-superday-interviews)). **These are not expected of 17-18-year-olds**, and no DA-route report mentions them. What school leavers are reported to face, or should be ready for:
- What does this division do? (FICC vs Equities; IBCM vs M&A; Operations)
- How does a bank make money? What happens to banks when interest rates rise?
- What is inflation, and what does the Bank of England do about it?
- A recent deal or market event and why it happened
- Simple FX or percentage maths, done out loud
- A logical puzzle (GS DA report)
- For markets roles: a simple stock or market pitch
- For tech roles: basic programming and problem solving

## 5. Implications for the app (JUDGEMENT)
1. Add **Superday mode** to mock interviews: 2-3 back-to-back 30-min sessions per firm preset (GS: CV + logic; JPM: motivation + technical).
2. Add a **logic-puzzle / think-out-loud** practice item type with model reasoning.
3. Add **"pitch me a stock / market" practice** with a school-leaver-level rubric (no DCF).
4. Group exercises can't be simulated honestly by AI; offer a **briefing + self-review checklist** and an "observer notes" example instead.
5. An **e-tray / in-tray** replica fits our test engine (prioritise and respond; mixed formats) and doubles as job-simulation prep for HSBC/KPMG/Deloitte.

## Design and UX (from screenshots)
- Clean, consistent, professional; good spacing; readable at 375px; sensible bottom navigation on phones.
- **First impression doesn't match the strategy**: the home page never mentions finance, banks or investment banking. Its stat "48 original practice questions" is out of date (the banks hold ~180 items).
- **Employer pages read like research notes**, not guidance for a 17-year-old (e.g. "Prep site: FICC often AAA… Treat as unofficial. One 2025 candidate applied with 37 IB points… salary GBP 24k-40k is a prep-site range"). Needs a plain-English summary on top (dates, pay, what you'd do, how to prepare), with the evidence underneath.
- Onboarding: home has a "Your prep" checklist (pick sector → test → interview → track → STAR story). Good. No finance-specific path.
- Empty states and errors: tracker/progress show helpful empty states; API errors show plain messages; the error page offers retry; a custom 404 exists.
- Loading: interview shows "Checking each answer…" messages; the site-wide "Loading…" placeholder is the CLS culprit above.

## Copy
- UK English throughout (spot checks found no US spellings in visible text).
- No over-promising: no "guaranteed offer"; terms say "We do not guarantee any outcome".
- "Unlimited" on Pro conflicts with the 150-a-day cap (see 2.6).
- Tone mostly suits 16-18s on the tool pages; employer pages are too dense (above).

## Score: **7 / 10** (vs Bright Network / Higherin polish and Intervyo SEO)
Reasons: very clean, accessible and error-free, but performance is below 90 on most pages from one fixable cause, employer pages share one title, the home page doesn't speak to finance, and employer pages are hard reading for the audience.

6. Structured data and finance SEO pages; check external links once web access exists.

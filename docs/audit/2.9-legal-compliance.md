# 2.9 Legal and compliance (UK; most users under 18): audit

Date: 2 October 2026. **This is not legal advice.** It's a checklist of what the site has and what looks missing, to take to a qualified lawyer. Pages reviewed: `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/pricing`, `/login`, `/tests`, `/mock`, footer, and the code behind them.

## What already exists (good)
- **Privacy notice** written for UK GDPR: controller, purposes and lawful bases (contract, legitimate interests), what's stored where (browser vs account), what goes to OpenAI (and moderation), voice/camera handling, processors with locations (OpenAI US, Supabase EU, Vercel, Stripe, Google), transfer safeguards, retention, rights incl. self-service deletion and export, ICO complaint route, and support numbers.
- **Terms**: independence and "not affiliated or endorsed" statement, accuracy caveats, AI-use responsibility, acceptable use, auto-renewal, cancellation, 14-day right with proportionate deduction, account deletion, England & Wales law, "nothing affects your statutory rights".
- **Cookies/PECR**: only strictly necessary storage; no analytics or ads; so no banner is required. Accurate today.
- **Children's Code basics**: no ads, no tracking, no profiling, no public content, data minimised, high privacy by default (local-only unless you sign in).
- **Disclaimers**: "independent, not affiliated with or endorsed by" in the footer, terms, `/tests` (names SHL, Aon, Civil Service) and `/mock`. Tests are labelled "SHL-style", "approximate". **No employer logos are used.**
- **Safeguarding signposting**: self-harm-related text triggers Childline / Samaritans / SHOUT / 999 instead of feedback.

## Gaps and risks

| # | Area | Gap | Who | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|

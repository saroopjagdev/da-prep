# 2.1 AI mock interviews: audit

Date: 2 October 2026. Checked by reading the code (`app/interview/page.tsx`, `lib/interview.ts`, `app/api/interview/*`, `lib/mockprocess/*`, `components/mock/*`) and by running full interviews in Chromium (desktop 1366px and mobile 375px) with the site's fake-AI mode (`MOCK_AI=1`).

**Not tested: the real AI.** No OpenAI key is available in this environment, so the quality, accuracy and consistency of real questions and feedback could not be measured. An evaluation set and runner are ready (below).

## What exists
Two interview features:
1. **Interview page (`/interview`)**: choose a sector (optional), paste a job advert (or use a sample), optionally paste a CV, pick an interview type (motivation / competency / strengths) and a format (typed, or "video style": spoken and transcribed). Five AI-generated questions, then AI marking (score per answer, STAR checklist, feedback, "stronger answer").
2. **Firm mock processes (`/mock/<firm>`)**: 8 firms (PwC, Deloitte, KPMG, EY, Barclays, Rolls-Royce, BAE Systems, Lloyds). Stage-by-stage replicas with per-stage preparation time, answer time and re-record limits, sourced reported questions plus original questions, scored against the firm's framework.

## Findings

| # | Finding | Evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Interview page is not firm- or role-specific.** Questions come from 15 fixed themes (`THEMES` in `lib/interview.ts`) plus whatever job advert is pasted. The firm profile data (stages, values, reported questions) is never passed in. Goldman Sachs Global Markets vs J.P. Morgan Operations vs Morgan Stanley Technology only differ if the student pastes a different advert | `nextQuestionSystem`, `nextQuestionUser` | High |
| 2 | **No commercial-awareness, technical or ethics questions** in the interview page (themes cover motivation, competency, strengths only). Research shows banks ask "a recent deal/market story", "how a bank makes money", FICC-type questions and integrity scenarios | `THEMES`; `docs/research/13-video-interviews.md` §3 | High (finance focus) |
| 3 | **Video mode doesn't match HireVue.** 60 seconds to answer (real: usually 2-3 min); no timed thinking period (real: ~30 s); no practice question; **unlimited "record again"** despite saying "one attempt, like the real thing"; one setting for all firms | `VIDEO_SECONDS = 60`; `recordAgain` always available in review state | High |
| 4 | Firm mock processes get timing right (e.g. PwC 30 s prep / 2 min answer / no re-records; re-record limits enforced in `QaStage.tsx`) **but cover no investment bank** (no GS, JPM, MS, BofA, DB, UBS, HSBC) | `lib/mockprocess/definitions.ts` | High (finance focus) |
| 5 | Marking rubric is STAR-centred: no explicit criteria for motivation, firm knowledge, commercial awareness or the firm's values; "improvements" exist but no concrete practice plan / next steps | `scoreSystem()` | Medium |
| 6 | Feedback tone guidance is good for teenagers ("5 is average for a teenager", "do not fabricate achievements, mark gaps with [add detail]") | `scoreSystem()` | Positive |
| 7 | Prompt-injection defences: user text is escaped so it can't close our tags (`esc`), and prompts say tagged text is "untrusted data". Moderation runs first (self-harm → Childline/Samaritans/SHOUT support message). Not tested live | `lib/prompt.ts`, `lib/server/safety.ts` | Positive (unverified) |
| 8 | Moderation **fails open**: if OpenAI's moderation is down, text goes through unscreened | `screenText` catch block | Low-Medium |
| 9 | Personal data in answers (names, addresses, phone numbers) is sent to OpenAI unredacted; no warning on the page | — | Medium (privacy, under-18s) |
| 10 | Non-English answers: no explicit handling; behaviour unknown until the eval runs | — | Low |
| 11 | **Free-limit bypass**: the free allowance is charged only when `history` is empty. A client can send a fake first turn and get unlimited free questions; `/api/interview/score` and `/api/star` don't count against any free allowance. Bounded only by the 150-calls-a-day cap (≈ £12/day/account worst case) | `app/api/interview/next/route.ts`, `score/route.ts`; `lib/server/usage.ts` | High (cost) |
| 12 | Error handling: browser gives up after 90 s with a clear message; server routes return friendly errors. OpenAI client timeout (default) is longer than the route's `maxDuration` (60-120 s), so slow calls end as platform timeouts | `lib/api.ts`; route files | Low |

## Evaluation set (built, not yet run)
- `evals/interview-cases.json`: **31 original answers** across GS Markets, JPM Operations, MS Technology, BofA Global Markets, HSBC Commercial Banking and PwC Audit: strong, average, weak, off-topic, a wrong technical answer, empty, rambling, three prompt-injection attempts, personal data, Spanish, offensive, and a distressed student.
- `evals/statement-cases.json`: 12 statements/answers (used in 2.4).
- `tests/evals.test.ts`: checks the files on every test run; with `RUN_EVALS=1` and an OpenAI key it marks every case twice, checks the score lands in the expected band, that two runs differ by at most 2 points, and that nothing leaks (system prompt, phone/address in the "better answer"). Results go to `evals/results/`. Estimated cost of a full run: well under £1.

## Score: **5 / 10** against the best competitor (Intervyo: firm-specific HireVue banks, per-firm settings, AI scoring on six competencies)
Reasons: solid engineering, safe tone and good mock-process timing, but the main interview page is generic, the video mode isn't HireVue-accurate, there are no investment-bank mocks or commercial-awareness questions, and the real AI is unmeasured.

## To reach 9+
1. Firm + programme picker on the interview page that feeds the firm profile (values, stages, reported question themes) into question generation and marking.
2. Question types for commercial awareness, technical (school-leaver level), "why apprenticeship", ethics; per-firm mixes.
3. HireVue mode with per-firm presets (think time, answer time, question count, retakes, practice question), reusing `QaStage` logic.
4. Mock processes for GS, JPM, MS, BofA, HSBC (and DB/UBS when researched).
5. Rubric with criteria (structure, specificity, motivation, firm knowledge, commercial awareness, values) and a "next 3 things to practise" plan.
6. Close the free-limit bypass and add a per-user daily cost budget.
7. Personal-data warning (and simple redaction of phone numbers/emails before sending).
8. Run the eval set with a real key and tune prompts until it passes.

## Score: **6 / 10** against the best competitor (JobTestPrep / Graduates First: large banks, employer-specific packs)
Reasons: every answer is correct, formats and timings are faithful and honestly labelled, and the review is good. But banks are small and fixed, several logic items are trivial or repetitive, Cappfinity and job-simulation formats (the most common in finance after SHL) are missing, and there are no finance scenarios.

## To reach 9+
1. Fix the deductive generator (never state the asked task's day; no duplicate rules; difficulty = number of deductions needed; step-by-step explanations) and remove the duplicate inductive item.
2. Fix the two debatable SJT keys.
3. Grow banks so retakes differ: generator-based numerical T/F and verbal (seeded per attempt), 3x the SJT scenarios including finance ones.
4. Build Cappfinity-style numerical/verbal/critical reasoning with time-recorded mode.
5. Build a job simulation / e-tray (inbox, mixed responses), reusable for HSBC/KPMG/Deloitte/EY mocks.
6. Full-length Aon scales; NatWest Work Scenarios format; SHL verbal.
7. Results: time per question, progress across attempts, and "practise next" suggestions.
